## Warehouse and Dispatch issue

The reported Warehouse-to-Dispatch case remains unresolved. Read-only checks
authenticated the Owner, Warehouse and Dispatch accounts. The Warehouse and
Dispatch accounts each had one eligible Nexa context, and their Tenant and
Workspace were confirmed equal. In that shared context, the Warehouse account
could list one ACTIVE warehouse; inventory lots, RESERVED inventory
reservations and canonical fulfillments each returned zero records. The
Dispatch account saw zero canonical readiness items, legacy reservation
readiness candidates and legacy dispatch orders. A separate Owner sales-order
query also returned zero; equality between the Owner and operational account
contexts was not checked. These results mean the reported workflow could not
be reproduced from available live records; they do not establish that a
specific previously observed order or reservation is fixed or explain why it
was not visible.

The UF-06 and UF-07 execution guide identifies missing visible lots and an
unidentified picking preparation. Valid lots, a confirmed order with inventory
backing, and a prepared Fulfillment/Physical Allocation may be needed to
exercise the chosen lifecycle. No live records were created or modified during
this diagnosis.

The current Mobile client lists picking work from `/api/v1/fulfillments` and
Dispatch readiness from `/api/v1/dispatch-readiness`. Those projections
require a current Fulfillment backed by a Physical Allocation and the
applicable active Warehouse grants. The API also has a legacy inventory
reservation flow exposed through different projections; the Mobile client
does not read that legacy list. The two lifecycles are not interchangeable.
Warehouse/Dispatch fixture tests exercise explicit grants and prepared
fulfillment state, but do not prove the missing runtime case or Render data.

No runtime root cause or fix is claimed, and no authorization or business
semantics were changed in this candidate. The signed APK is prepared for the
Owner-authorized controlled direct distribution described below; the absence
of a reproducible live order does not convert the technical CI result into
Product or System Acceptance.
